# Canonical Admissibility Relation

Systemic Admissibility is expressed through a minimal canonical relation:

\[
A = \frac{V}{E + I + S}
\]

This expression is not an equation of motion, utility, or optimization.  
It is a **structural constraint**.

## Term Definitions

- **V (Value):** The functional outcome that justifies persistence  
- **E (Energy):** Physical, metabolic, or operational cost  
- **I (Information):** Control, coordination, and informational burden  
- **S (Structure):** Scale, organization, and structural overhead  

## Interpretation

- Increasing **V** without accounting for **E + I + S** produces instability
- Systems collapse when costs outpace their capacity to integrate them
- Admissibility fails **before** failure becomes observable

The relation is **dimension-agnostic** and **domain-independent**.
It serves as a diagnostic lens, not a computational rule.
