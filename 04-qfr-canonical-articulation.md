# Quaternion Field Recursion (QFR) — Canonical Articulation

The work of R. Wade H. Marr (aka “Hunter Wade”) on Quaternion Field Recursion (QFR)
is included here as a **canonical articulation**, not as an operational model.

QFR does not introduce new dynamics, algorithms, or mechanisms.
It formalizes the **grammatical level** at which fields:

- close on themselves
- preserve coherence
- enforce admissibility prior to execution

Within this framework, QFR is treated as:
- a clarification of closure
- a rigorous expression of recursion
- a non-operational lens on admissibility

No executable dependency is created.
No predictive or optimization role is implied.

QFR strengthens the canonical layer by articulating
*why* closure precedes dynamics,
without specifying *how* dynamics unfold.
